Personal Goal

My dream is to develop construction robots. I believe civil engineering is one of the most underdeveloped yet crucial fields.
In Korea, the construction workforce is steadily declining, and I think robots must step in to fill this gap.

Moreover, if humanity is to build structures in space, the development of construction robots will be essential.
This is the work I truly aspire to do, and to achieve it, I am currently deeply interested in robot learning.
