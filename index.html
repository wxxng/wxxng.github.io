Research Interests

I'm interested in enabling humanoid robots to perform complex tasks in unstructured and dynamic environments, particularly in construction. My research interests include loco-manipulation, dexterous manipulation, and scene understanding—capabilities that allow robots to navigate, perceive, and physically interact with complex surroundings.

Ultimately, I hope to develop humanoid robots that can work reliably alongside people in construction environments.
